About Marium Hammad

Mixed media and resin artist in Calgary, Alberta

Download resume (PDF)

I create original handmade artwork that combines texture, colour, and decorative design to produce visually engaging pieces suitable for contemporary interiors and creative spaces. My work is inspired by colour, texture, nature, and contemporary interior spaces, and currently focuses on resin geode artwork, botanical textured art, Islamic-inspired art, and mixed-media decorative pieces.

I bring documentation, organization, customer service and digital-content skills from professional and community experience, and I am building an authentic portfolio alongside my studio work.
